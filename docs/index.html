Method and limits

  • Data: 1,067,371 invoice lines from a UK online gift retailer, December 2009 to December 2011. Duplicates, cancellations, non-product codes and invalid prices are removed before analysis.
  • Model: gradient boosted trees on eleven features describing the previous 12 months, validated out of time. The trees are exported to JSON and scored in this page.
  • The test period is the seasonal peak, so predicted probabilities run low even though the ranking holds.
  • Predicting who will buy is not the same as knowing whom a campaign will persuade. That needs a randomised test.
